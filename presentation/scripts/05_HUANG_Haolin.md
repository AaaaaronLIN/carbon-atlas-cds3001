## D. Q&A 主答題目

1. 「廣告怎麼做的？」→ *"We wrote all three scripts ourselves and produced the poster from our own HTML/CSS. The audio and the video are produced from those scripts — drafts, not an agency production — and all three carry the same line: 37,856 million tonnes a year. Now find the part you can move."*
2. 「下一步做什麼？」→ *"Automatic refresh from Carbon Monitor; sub-national data; then a per-capita view, but only with a population source we can cite. None of those are built, and we are not claiming them."*
3. 「為什麼選這三個媒體／為什麼這樣拍？」→ *"Poster stops people on a wall; audio gives one sentence to remember; video proves the dashboard is real. One message, three media — not three messages."*
4. 「產品值得付錢嗎？」→ 與 WENG 分工：*"We do not claim revenue or clients — it was never sold. The model is one analyst saving two hours a week: HK$23,000 a year against a HK$31,746 one-off, break-even about 17 months. The value is the hours plus the caveats being visible."*
5. 「為什麼 demo 是錄影？」→ 交 LAI 主答；你可補一句：*"The deck and the campaign are built for any classroom laptop — the recording makes the demo playable anywhere."*
